# Erata — erataofficial.art
## Website proposal + working prototype

**Prepared:** 26 October 2026  
**Domain:** [erataofficial.art](https://erataofficial.art)  
**Status:** Concept, content architecture, and a responsive front-end prototype

---

## 01 — In one page

Erata needs a home that feels like the records: singular, tactile, a little mysterious, and unmistakably built around the mark **Ợ**.

This proposal turns the symbol into the site's organising idea. The supplied Ợ mark is centred at the top of the page with the navigation beneath it; in the hero, the artist portrait becomes the circle and a single black dot closes the mark again. The same ring-and-dot rhythm runs through the player, the section language, the contact page, and the social preview. The result is a quiet, paper-and-ink artist site that lets the music and the artwork do the loudest work.

The prototype is a single, fast, responsive page:

- a portrait-led introduction;
- a five-record featured player powered by SoundCloud;
- a six-project portfolio;
- a complete release-level discography (20 releases, 31 SoundCloud uploads);
- the project's own language around **Ợ / Circle In The Flow**, synesthesia, **Artosm**, cinematic and complextro sound;
- direct contact and streaming links;
- no trackers, no cookie banner, no stock imagery, and no dependency on Linktree.

The current domain is a Squarespace “Coming Soon” page. The proposal includes a low-risk route to replace it while preserving the **info@erataofficial.art** mailbox.

The site should not add another competing visual language. It should act as the frame around these artefacts.

## 03 — The idea

### Circle In The Flow

The Ợ mark is already a complete graphic system: a ring, a tail, a dot. The site treats it as **architecture**, not as a logo pasted into the corner.

Three moves define the experience:

1. **The portrait becomes the mark.**  
   In the hero, the wide banner portrait is cropped into a circle. A dot sits beneath it. The page opens by completing the Ợ symbol with the artist inside it.

2. **The site is a printed object that happens to play.**  
   Warm paper, black ink, hairline rules, a mono metadata layer, and a single signal red. No gradients-as-decoration, no glassmorphism, no stock photography, no generic “creative studio” layout.

3. **The discography is an index, not a shop window.**  
   The portfolio has room to breathe. The complete archive is a precise, utilitarian list. The two are deliberately different rhythms.

The name of the concept comes directly from the artist's own language: **Circle In The Flow**. It is not an invented campaign; it is the meaning Erata has already given the mark.

### Why minimalism

Minimalism here is not an aesthetic shortcut. It is a way to make the work legible:

- one portrait, because one strong image is enough;
- one accent colour, because the artwork already supplies colour;
- one page, because the story is continuous;
- one player, because the SoundCloud catalogue is the source of truth;
- no autoplay, no pop-ups, no cookie wall, no carousel that moves without consent.

The result should feel like an artist's edition, not a template.

## 04 — What the visitor experiences

### 4.1 Masthead — the mark, centred

- The supplied **Ợ mark**, centred and enlarged, with no accompanying wordmark.
- The primary navigation directly beneath the mark: **Listen / Works / Index / World / Contact**.
- Larger nav type than the first draft; the header stays centred at every breakpoint.
- The header is fixed and changes from transparent to a blurred paper or ink bar as the page scrolls, switching to light type over the dark sections.

### 4.2 Hero — the mark completed

- A thin editorial top line: **Yiğit Erata — Ankara, TR** / **Est. 2015 — Ợ**.
- A very large **ERATA** wordmark, set tight and flush to the left.
- A circular, grayscale portrait with a dot beneath it.
- A role line: **Composer / Producer / Sound Designer**.
- A style line: **Cinematic / Complextro / Drum & Bass. Sound for synesthesia.**
- Two actions: **Play latest** and **Explore the index**.
- A live Ankara clock and a scroll cue.

The first mobile view is tuned so the portrait, the intro, and **Play latest** all remain visible without a scroll on a 390 × 844 viewport.

### 4.3 Listen — selected transmissions

A dark, cinematic chamber inside the paper site.

- Five featured releases: *Zenith*, *Artosm's Dream Diaries*, *LIMINAL*, *The Portalist*, and *More Than One*.
- A large cover, title, credit, short factual description, release date, genre, and duration.
- A row of cover tabs to change the selected release.
- A custom minimal player: cover, title, transport controls, elapsed time, seek bar, and SoundCloud attribution.
- Audio is loaded from SoundCloud **only after the visitor presses play**. The iframe is not present on first load.

### 4.4 Portfolio — selected works

Six projects across remix, album, EP, compilation, and label releases:

1. *Zenith (Erata Remix)* — for Mefjus & flowanastasia, 2026
2. *Split From The Past* — album, 2025
3. *Synesthete* — Rebel Music, 2024
4. *The Portalist* — Full Flex Audio, 2024
5. *Born With Light* — EP, 2023
6. *Artosm's Dream Diaries* — Turkstep / Baskı I, 2026

The asymmetric, staggered grid keeps the page from reading like a product catalogue. Each cover links to its source.

### 4.5 Index — the full discography

- 20 releases, 31 SoundCloud uploads, 2020–2026.
- Filters: **All / Albums + EPs / Singles / Remixes / Other**.
- Each row: year, artwork, title, credit, format, duration, play (where a SoundCloud track exists), and an external link.
- A persistent line to the full SoundCloud archive.
- Rows are real HTML content, so the catalogue is visible without JavaScript and readable by search engines.

### 4.6 World — the story

A dark chapter that uses the supplied extended biography as its spine:

- **Circle in the flow.**
- **Pt. 1 — Intro:** Yiğit Erata, composer, producer and sound designer, from the heart of Anatolia: Ankara.
- **Pt. 2 — Universe:** concepts, universes and lore; the inner world reflected outward; where inner worlds unite, they end up in Circle In The Flow.
- **Pt. 3 — Approach:** music production as filmmaking; catching the motions of sound; making it possible to live dreams in real life.
- **Origin:** Ankara, Õ Protocol, sound as a way to understand human psychology and human creations, and Artosm as the mind's understanding of emotion.
- The pull quote: *“There is much more undiscovered than discovered. Circle In The Flow is aware of that…”*
- A three-part glossary: **Ợ / Circle In The Flow**, **Artosm**, **Synesthesia**.
- A full-width portrait strip with restrained captions.

This is the page that turns a discography into a world. It now uses the artist's own supplied text and no longer needs to be marked as provisional.

### 4.7 Contact

- Direct email: **info@erataofficial.art**
- A clear line: “For composition, production, sound design, remixes, licensing and press.”
- SoundCloud, Spotify, Instagram, YouTube, X, and Discord.
- Location: Ankara, Turkey.
- A quiet, oversized Ợ mark closing the page.

### 4.8 Persistent player

The player docks to the bottom of the viewport after playback begins. It can pause, skip to the next or previous playable release, seek, link back to SoundCloud, and close. It is designed to stay out of the way, with a single live dot marking playback.

### 4.9 Mobile behaviour

- The portrait shrinks to keep the primary play action in the first view.
- The logo and the full five-link navigation stay visible under it, scaled down rather than hidden behind a menu button.
- The works become a single editorial column.
- The discography becomes a compact two-column row layout with year, title, artwork, format, duration, and actions.
- Filters scroll horizontally or wrap into a clear two-column grid.
- No horizontal page overflow.

## 05 — Design system

| Element | Decision |
|---|---|
| Canvas | `#E9E8E3` warm paper |
| Ink | `#0B0B0A` near-black |
| Muted text | `#62615A` (5.07:1 on paper) |
| Signal | `#C22612` light / `#FF6A55` dark |
| Display | **Inter Tight** — tight, modern, neutral |
| Editorial accent | **Instrument Serif Italic** — used sparingly for the “flow” of the page |
| Metadata | **DM Mono** — catalogue numbers, dates, labels, player time |
| Mark | The supplied transparent Ợ artwork, used as a CSS mask so it can be black or paper while preserving the exact drawing |
| Imagery | The artist's portrait and release artwork only; no stock imagery |
| Grid | 12 columns, 1680px max, fluid 20–64px gutters |
| Motion | Short reveals, a slow ticker, quiet hover states, a live player dot; no autoplaying carousel; reduced-motion support |
| Corners | Square controls, circular mark and portrait; no soft UI pills |
| Voice | Spare, precise, artist-led; no hype language |

### Mark usage

The Ợ symbol is the one non-negotiable asset. The site never redraws it casually. The avatar has been converted into a transparent black mark for flexible use, with the original image retained as the source.

- Masthead: 72px-high mark, centred, with no wordmark; the nav sits directly beneath it.
- Hero: the mark is recreated at architectural scale by the portrait and dot.
- Contact: a large, 12%-opacity mark as a closing signature.
- Favicon and social card: the mark isolated on black.

## 06 — Content model

Each release uses the following fields:

```text
id, year, title, shortTitle, credit, label, format, group,
date, duration, artwork (900 + 480), SoundCloud URL,
external URL, service, short description
```

Editorial rules for the final copy:

- Use **Erata** and he/him pronouns, as confirmed in the extended biography.
- Do not use unverified biography or third-party AI-written artist descriptions.
- Keep descriptions factual: collaborators, label, format, and release context.
- Use the artist's own phrases — *Circle In The Flow*, *Artosm*, *synesthesia* — with care and attribution.
- Mark free downloads only where the artist has published them as such.
- Confirm the final *Split From The Past* tracklist and credits before adding a detailed album expansion.

The prototype has no fake news, no invented awards, no invented monthly listeners, and no invented live dates.

## 07 — Technical approach

### 7.1 Build

- Static HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript.
- No framework, no bundler, no runtime dependencies.
- All fonts and images are self-hosted.
- The catalogue is real HTML for SEO and no-JavaScript reading.
- Content data lives in `assets/js/data.js`; the index rows are authored in the HTML so they remain visible without JavaScript.

### 7.2 Audio

- On first load the experience is image and type only. SoundCloud is not contacted until a play control is used.
- Playback uses the official **SoundCloud Widget API** in a hidden iframe.
- No API keys or client secrets are exposed.
- Every play control is a normal link to SoundCloud when JavaScript is unavailable.
- The player falls back to a clear “Open on SoundCloud” route if the embed cannot load.
- The site does not download or re-host audio.

### 7.3 Performance

Targets for the production build:

- Largest Contentful Paint under **1.5s** on a typical 4G connection.
- Cumulative Layout Shift under **0.05**.
- Initial JavaScript under **50KB** uncompressed (roughly 15KB compressed) before the SoundCloud widget is requested.
- Hero image and display font preloaded; below-fold imagery lazy-loaded.
- WebP artwork at 900px and 480px; responsive `srcset` for the portrait.
- No third-party scripts on first load.

### 7.4 Privacy

- No analytics, advertising, or social embeds on first load.
- No cookie banner is required for the current build.
- If analytics are added, use a cookieless service (Plausible, Fathom, or Cloudflare Web Analytics) with explicit event names.
- SoundCloud only receives a request after the visitor presses play. Once loaded, SoundCloud's own player and cookie policy apply inside that embed.

### 7.5 Performance and technical QA already performed

- Responsive checks at 1440, 1280, 1024, 900, 768 and 390px widths.
- No horizontal overflow.
- Filters and featured-release switching verified.
- SoundCloud playback verified end-to-end in a real browser engine, including with the production CSP headers.
- No uncaught JavaScript exceptions.
- No broken local assets.
- No-JavaScript rendering verified: all content remains visible and all source links work.
- Automated axe-core 4.10.2 scan returned no violations on desktop, mobile, the open player, and the 404 page.

## 08 — Accessibility & SEO

### Accessibility

- Semantic landmarks and a single H1.
- Skip link.
- Visible focus states.
- Keyboard-operable navigation, tabs, filters, player, and seek control.
- A persistent mobile navigation that keeps all five sections reachable without a menu overlay.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` support.
- Alt text for portraits and artwork.
- Text contrast adjusted to meet WCAG AA for normal text, including muted copy and signal red.
- No autoplaying audio.
- External links clearly labelled.
- The player announces state changes through a polite live region.

### SEO

- Descriptive title and meta description.
- Canonical URL.
- Open Graph and Twitter card with a custom 1200 × 630 image.
- `MusicGroup` JSON-LD with genre, founding location, logo, email, and `sameAs` links.
- XML sitemap.
- `robots.txt`.
- Semantic release content in HTML.
- Custom 404 page with a route back into the site.
- Security and caching headers in `_headers`.

## 09 — Domain & launch plan

The current `erataofficial.art` is a Squarespace “Coming Soon” page. The new site is static and can be hosted on Cloudflare Pages, Netlify, Vercel, or any static host.

**Recommended:** Cloudflare Pages, because it gives fast global delivery, free TLS, simple custom domains, and the `_headers` / `_redirects` files already included in the prototype.

### Suggested launch sequence

1. **Approve content and rights.** Confirm pronouns, contact language, artwork permissions, label credits, and the exact bio.
2. **Freeze the DNS snapshot.** Export all MX, SPF, DKIM, DMARC, and verification records before touching nameservers.
3. **Deploy to a preview URL.** Test the player, forms, social cards, 404, and mobile behaviour.
4. **Connect the domain.** Add `erataofficial.art` and `www.erataofficial.art` to the static host. Redirect `www` to the apex.
5. **Preserve email.** Recreate the existing mail records exactly; verify `info@erataofficial.art` before retiring the old site.
6. **Lower TTL, then switch.** Keep the Squarespace site available until the new domain resolves correctly everywhere.
7. **Post-launch checks.** SSL, canonical, sitemap submission, social previews, page speed, accessibility, and a test email.
8. **Retire the old page** only after the mailbox and DNS have been stable for at least a week.

The commercial details of the domain and email provider need to be confirmed before this sequence is executed.

## 10 — Measurement

Suggested events, if analytics are approved:

- `play_release` — release ID, source (hero / featured / index)
- `outbound_stream` — SoundCloud or Spotify, release ID
- `email_click`
- `section_reach` — Listen, Works, Index, World, Contact
- `filter_use`

Useful questions:

- Which releases drive the most plays and outbound clicks?
- Do visitors move from the featured release into the full index?
- Does the contact route get used?
- Which social source brings the highest-quality listening sessions?

The prototype does not install analytics. Adding it should be a separate, explicit decision.
