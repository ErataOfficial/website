02 29 October / Republic Day

Why 29 October matters.

Ankara, 29.10.1923

A future where every voice can be heard.

On 29 October 1923, Mustafa Kemal Atatürk imagined a country where the future would belong to its people. Where knowledge, freedom, culture and art could flourish. With the founding of the Republic, he helped open a new chapter in which generations could learn, create, question and express themselves without limits.

He understood that a nation’s voice lives not only in its history, but also in its art. He championed the development of music education and the institutions that would give future generations of artists the opportunity to find their own voices.

A century later, that vision continues to resonate.

Every melody we write, every rhythm we create, every stage we step onto carries a little of that legacy. Artists like Erata are part of a generation that can explore, experiment and make its voice heard because a path was opened long before us. They (Erata and other artists) walk towards the darkness and the unknown, carrying in their hands the torches lit by Atatürk.

Without that beginning, our music might never have found its way into the world.

Today, we celebrate not only the founding of a Republic, but the freedom to imagine, create and be heard.

Happy 29 October Republic Day!

04 World

Circle in the flow.

Pt. 1 Intro

Erata is a genreless electronic / bass music project and the work of Yiğit Erata, a producer, sound designer and DJ from the heart of Anatolia: Ankara, Turkey. His music is a hybrid style spanning cinematic, complextro and drum & bass: abstract, experimental, cinematic, synesthete.

Pt. 2 Universe

With unique concepts, universes and lore, Erata has become a dynamic figure in electronic music. It is all about reflecting the inner world and emotion outward. Where inner worlds unite, they end up united in Circle In The Flow, also known as the Erata logo.

Pt. 3 Approach

Erata approaches music production like making a film, seeing music's scenario and transformative power. Catch the motions of sounds, bring them into our world, and make it possible to live dreams in real life. As he continues pushing electronic music's boundaries, he remains committed to artistic integrity, inviting listeners on a journey through sound.

Origin Õ Protocol / Artosm

Born into a dusty, crowded, faded-gray city, Ankara. At ten, he started the project Õ Protocol, making productions from the sounds in his head. Years passed, and he discovered the sound of the environment: a way to describe and understand human psychology, the nature of humans, and human creations.

It is not just about making music. Artosm is the state of how the human mind understands and is aware of emotions. Conscious, he began wondering how much we are capable of under a heavily edited emotion by ourselves.

“There is much more undiscovered than discovered. Circle In The Flow is aware of that…”
